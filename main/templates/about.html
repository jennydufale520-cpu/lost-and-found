About Us

Lost & Found is a platform designed to help reunite lost items with their rightful owners.

Our Mission

To create a trusted, easy-to-use community space where lost belongings can be reclaimed. Every item matters — whether it's a phone, ID card, wallet, or keys.

How It Works

  1. Someone finds an item → posts it here
  2. Someone loses an item → browses the listings
  3. They connect → item is returned!
